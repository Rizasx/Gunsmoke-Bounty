Gunsmoke Bounty privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Gunsmoke Bounty has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours. Your progress, settings and unlocks are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage.

Purchases

Expansion packs are bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and shares with the app only whether a pack has been purchased. We never see your payment details.

Fonts

The game loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when the game starts, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

Children

The game is a cartoon-style western with stylised gunfights. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, and all saved progress is removed from your device.
